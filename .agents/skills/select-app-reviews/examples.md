# Review quote shape

Invented indie-app wording for `displayQuote` only. Not real reviews. Prefer 20–40 words; allow 15–45. Standalone, specific workflow or benefit. No financial, plan, or competitor language. No faint praise, backhanded or complaint-adjacent framing, stitching, or ellipses.

## Accepted

Complete body, prefer 20–40:

- `I used to lose drafts between my phone and laptop. Now I start a note on the train and finish it at my desk without hunting through folders. It just stays where I left it.`
- `The lock-screen widget is the only reason I actually log water. I tap it between meetings and the streak stays honest without opening the app every time.`
- `Exporting a week of notes used to mean ten minutes of copy-paste. One tap and I have a clean PDF I can send before standup starts.`

Complete body at the 15-word floor (keep when specific):

- `I start a note on my phone and pick it up on my Mac later.`

Faithful contiguous excerpt from a longer body (`adaptation.type` includes `excerpted`; no ellipses). Source: `I used to keep three note apps and a desktop folder just to find last week’s draft. Now I start on the train and finish at my desk without hunting through folders, and the copy is always the one I expect.` Display:

- `Now I start on the train and finish at my desk without hunting through folders, and the copy is always the one I expect.`

## Rejected

- `Great app! Really useful and well designed. Highly recommend to anyone looking for something like this.` — faint praise; no specific workflow or benefit.
- `Worth every penny of the yearly plan, especially compared with the free version of the other apps I tried.` — plan and competitor language.
- `Finally works after the last update. Still crashes if I have too many notes, but better than it was.` — backhanded and complaint-adjacent.
- `Love the sync… and the widgets… and how fast search feels now.` — stitched with ellipses; not one standalone excerpt.

Translated, then still omit (do not recommend; language is not the omission reason):

- Source: `Tolle App, macht genau was sie soll. Sehr empfehlenswert!` Display: `Great app, does exactly what it should. Highly recommended!` — faint praise after a faithful translation.
