# Public copy standard

Use for drafting, editing, implementation microcopy, and copy critique.

- Start from one audience, problem, supported promise, differentiator, and action.
- Name the app's actual action, mechanism, state, or supported outcome. Rewrite or cut a sentence that could describe an unrelated app unchanged.
- Revalidate every inherited visitor-facing string. A starter sentence is not safe merely because it omits the starter name; replace domain-mismatched language and generic headings that do not express a supported target-app action, benefit, or distinction.
- Except for compact structural labels such as `Good to know`, each homepage section heading must identify the product domain, a user action or state, or a supported distinction when read by itself. Nearby cards do not rescue an interchangeable heading; pronoun-only promises such as "everything you need" or "everything you want" fail this test.
- Section headings are labels, not sentences. Never end them with a period. This rule does not apply to the hero or closing CTA headlines.
- `The essentials, thoughtfully covered` is starter-only copy and must not survive initialization. Replace it with a target-specific heading even when the surrounding cards are correct.
- Prefer concrete benefits over feature inventories. Keep one idea per sentence, card, and section.
- Use the brief's terminology, capitalization, person, and credible customer situations.
- Remove puffery, vague attribution, unsupported intensifiers, generic SaaS language, rhetorical setup, forced lists, slogan fragments, synthetic enthusiasm, and interchangeable claims.
- Avoid em dashes in visitor copy. Split the sentence or use a comma.
- Never add a number, timeframe, superlative, guarantee, urgency, privacy reassurance, or outcome without exact support.
- Keep decision-critical limits beside the affected claim or CTA. Keep provenance and implementation notes private unless omission would mislead.
- Answer recorded objections with verified facts. Do not convert features into fake FAQ questions.
- Metadata should read naturally and match the visible page. Do not write keyword lists or copy solely for schema.
- Card copy must remain scannable within existing component geometry. Change wording, not card width, typography, or spacing.
- Blog and Changelog are permanent infrastructure, not optional copy sections. Never describe their routes or links as omitted because their collections are empty.

## Diagnostic examples

These examples demonstrate corrections, not facts or phrases to reuse. Replace their domain, action, and level of certainty with wording supported by the current brief.

| Problem                 | Weak                                | Better when supported                                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vague benefit           | `Organize everything effortlessly.` | `Group saved recipes into collections you can rename and reorder.`                       |
| Feature inventory       | `Tags, filters, widgets, and sync.` | `Filter saved recipes by collection or tag, then open them from the Home Screen widget.` |
| Interchangeable heading | `Everything you need`               | `Keep saved recipes sorted by meal and cuisine`                                          |
| Misleading CTA          | `Start saving time`                 | `Download on the App Store`                                                              |
| Unsupported certainty   | `Never lose a recipe again.`        | `Save recipes so you can return to them later.`                                          |

Before handoff, read the visitor narrative aloud, trace every material public claim to the brief, and remove repetition.
