## Personally-identifying information

{appName} does not gather or transmit any personally-identifying information.

Syncing is handled securely through your iCloud account. Downloads and payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies to data handled by Apple.

## Analytics

{appName} uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-first analytics service that collects anonymous usage data to help guide development. It is designed not to identify individual users. See [TelemetryDeck's privacy information](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

## Payment data

In-app purchases are managed with [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com). If you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives a receipt about that purchase. It does not receive or process your payment details, such as a credit card number. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
